Terms of Service

The fine print, written plainly.

These are the terms that govern every standard and custom order. If anything here is unclear, email us before you place a deposit.

On this page Pricing & Quotations Customization Policy Payment Terms Lead Time & Delivery Quality Assurance & Warranty FAQ

1. Pricing & Quotations

  • Variable pricing. All standard product prices vary depending on the specific weaving style, material grade, and frame structure chosen.
  • Price flexibility. All listed prices are negotiable.
  • Validity of quotes. Quotation inquiries are handled by email. Formal price quotations are valid for 30 days from the date of issue, after which they may change based on material costs.

2. Customization Policy

We warmly welcome custom designs built to your space and aesthetic.

  • Design submission. Send custom designs, blueprints, or reference photos by email at sevineilfurniture@gmail.com or via Messenger.
  • Specification requirements. Include your preferred dimensions, synthetic rattan color/texture, and foam fabric so we can quote accurately.
  • Approval process. Once production has commenced, custom orders cannot be altered or canceled.

3. Payment Terms

  • Deposit. A non-refundable deposit of 50% is required to start production on both standard and customized orders. Deposit amounts can also be negotiated.
  • Balance payment. The remaining 50% is due in full prior to, or on the day of, delivery or pickup.
  • Accepted methods. Bank transfer via BPI, plus GCash and GoTyme e-wallets.

4. Lead Time & Delivery

  • Production schedule. Standard orders typically take 2–4 weeks. Custom designs may need an additional 1–2 weeks depending on complexity.
  • Delivery logistics. Shipping runs through Lalamove and private transport. Fees are calculated separately and covered by the customer unless a promotion states otherwise — and, like our other terms, delivery fees are negotiable.
  • Inspection upon receipt. Inspect your furniture immediately on delivery. Report any transit damage or discrepancies within 48 hours, with photographic evidence.

5. Quality Assurance & Warranty

  • Weather resistance. Our synthetic rattan is weatherproof and designed for indoor and outdoor use. Normal wear from extreme, prolonged weather exposure sits outside the structural warranty.
  • Warranty coverage. A 6-month limited warranty covers manufacturing defects in frame structural integrity and rattan unraveling. It does not cover damage from misuse, accidents, or improper cleaning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the 50% deposit refundable?

No. The deposit is non-refundable once it's paid, since it starts production. The deposit amount itself, however, can be negotiated before you pay it.

Can I change or cancel my order after I've paid the deposit?

Once production has commenced, custom and standard orders can't be altered or canceled. Get your dimensions, rattan color, and fabric confirmed before the deposit goes in.

Where do you deliver?

We currently serve and deliver to Luzon only, using Lalamove and private transport. Fees are calculated separately from the furniture price and are the customer's responsibility unless a promotion says otherwise.

How long will my order take?

Standard orders run 2–4 weeks. Fully custom designs typically add another 1–2 weeks, depending on how complex the build is.

How do I pay, and when?

Half the total (negotiable) is due upfront to start the build, via BPI bank transfer, GCash, or GoTyme. The remaining half is due before or on the day of delivery or pickup.

My piece arrived damaged — what do I do?

Inspect it the moment it arrives. Report any damage or discrepancy within 48 hours with clear photos, so we can sort it out quickly.

What does the warranty actually cover?

Six months against manufacturing defects — frame structural issues and rattan unraveling. It doesn't extend to misuse, accidents, improper cleaning, or normal wear from sustained extreme weather exposure.

Can I submit my own design or reference photo?

Yes — that's most of what we do. Email it to sevineilfurniture@gmail.com or send it via Messenger, along with your preferred dimensions, rattan color/texture, and cushion fabric.

Is this furniture actually okay left outdoors?

Yes, the synthetic rattan is weatherproof and built for indoor or outdoor placement. Extended extreme weather exposure causing normal wear over time isn't covered by the structural warranty.
