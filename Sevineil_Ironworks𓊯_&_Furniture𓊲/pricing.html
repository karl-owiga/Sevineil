How pricing works

Variable by design, transparent by policy

Variable pricing

Standard prices shift with weaving style, material grade, and frame structure. The listed price is a starting point for that build, not a fixed catalog rate.

Everything is negotiable

Listed prices, deposits, and delivery fees can all be discussed — tell us your budget and space, and we'll tell you what's possible.

Quotes hold for 30 days

Formal quotations are sent by email and stay valid for 30 days from issue. After that, material costs may push the number.

The made-to-order process

From reference photo to delivery day

Custom designs are welcome — send dimensions, blueprints, or reference photos and we'll build against them.

  1. 01

    Send your brief

    Email sevineilfurniture@gmail.com or message us — include preferred dimensions, rattan color/texture, and cushion fabric.

  2. 02

    Get your quote

    We confirm scope and send a formal quotation by email, valid for 30 days. Pricing and deposit terms can still be negotiated at this stage.

  3. 03

    Lock in with a 50% deposit

    A deposit — standard 50%, negotiable — starts production. This deposit is non-refundable once the build begins, and orders can't be altered or canceled after that point.

  4. 04

    We build

    Standard orders take 2–4 weeks. Custom designs add roughly 1–2 weeks depending on complexity.

  5. 05

    Settle the balance & receive

    The remaining 50% is due on or before delivery/pickup. Inspect your piece on arrival — flag transit damage within 48 hours, with photos.
